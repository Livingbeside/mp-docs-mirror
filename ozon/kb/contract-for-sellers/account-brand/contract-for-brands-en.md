# Trademark Holder Agreement

_Главная / Договоры для Продавцов на платформе Ozon / Кабинет бренда_

This revision is effective as of  February 19, 2026. We publish amendments in advance: if the rules on this page are not already in effect, see the [previous version](https://seller-edu.ozon.ru/contract-for-sellers/account-brand/spisok-izmenenii-contract-for-brands).

By accepting the Trademark Holder Agreement (hereinafter referred to as the Agreement), the Right Holder confirms that they have read its text and fully understand its terms and conditions. If the Right Holder disagrees with the terms and conditions, they are obliged to refuse to enter into the Agreement.

The Agreement forms an offer in accordance with Article 435 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation, comes into force from its publication at [/contract-for-sellers/account-brand/contract-for-brands](https://seller-edu.ozon.ru/contract-for-sellers/account-brand/contract-for-brands) and shall continue for an indefinite period.

The Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Russian Federation.

The Agreement contains clauses with active hyperlinks to legal documents that form an integral part of the Agreement, unless indicated otherwise. The Russian version of the Agreement shall prevail.

# 1. Conclusion, amendment and termination of the Agreement

1.1. The Agreement shall be concluded by the Right Holder for an indefinite period by clicking the "Accept Offer" button in the Brand PA, sending the text of the Agreement with the Activation Notice to Ozon any subsequent Rights Holder’s actions in the Brand PA to fulfill the terms of the Agreement within 365 calendar days from the activation date. The Agreement is recognized as concluded on the date of sending the Activation Notice.

1.2. Ozon may refuse to activate and conclude the Agreement by sending a Notice if the Right Holder provided details with errors or failed to pass the security check due to violation of agreements previously concluded with Ozon, or attempted fraud and/or any other violation of law. The potential Right Holder has the right to challenge the refusal of Activation by providing evidence of the absence of reasons for the refusal, and Ozon is obliged to consider the objection and provide a reasoned response within 7 calendar days. Ozon has the right not to consider the objection on similar grounds in the absence of additional evidence and information.

1.3. Ozon has the right to unilaterally change the terms of the Agreement by notifying the Right Holder at least 7 calendar days before the changes come into force. The Right Holder is responsible for monitoring the Notices and, within 7 calendar days of their receipt, may send Ozon a request to terminate the Agreement. In the absence of such request, the changes shall be deemed agreed upon by the Parties and shall apply from the date of coming into force.

1.4. Ozon and the Right Holder have the right to repudiate the Agreement by sending a Notice.

1.5. The date of termination of the Agreement shall be:

(a) if terminated at the initiative of the Right Holder with a valid Subscription – the last day of the current access period;

(b) in other cases – the day of the Notice.

1.6. Violations committed by the Right Holder shall provide grounds for termination of the Agreement by Ozon and shall be specified in the Notice:

(a) violation of the terms of the Section “Standard Clauses”;

(b) activity in the Brand PA indicates that the Right Holder collects data and/or obtains access to them automatically or by using third-party software;

(c) attempted fraud or any other violation of law;

(d) violation of payment obligations under the Agreement;

(e) repeated (more than 2 times) infringement of intellectual or other rights of third parties;

(f) violation of the [Rules for Communicating with Ozon Clients](https://seller-edu.ozon.ru/policies/rules/communication).

The Right Holder may challenge repudiation of the Agreement by Ozon by providing evidence of the non-violation, and Ozon is obliged to consider the objection within 7 calendar days. If Ozon has provided a reasoned refusal to the objection, Ozon has the right not to consider subsequent objections on the same grounds in the absence of additional evidence and information.

# 2. Subject Matter

(a) paid activation of the Premium Brand Subscription (including, but not limited to, if the Premium Brand Subscription is part of the Premium Plus or Premium Pro service) – a set of services rendered to the Right Holder in the Brand PA during the access period;

(b) provision by Ozon of additional services to the Right Holder.

The Agreement also governs the terms and conditions for accessing and using specific functionalities of the Brand PA.

2.2. To activate the Subscription, the Right Holder has to enter into an Agreement, register and log in the Brand PA as well as undergo the procedure for verifying documents confirming their right to the Trademark protection.

2.3. After undergoing the procedure for verifying documents confirming their right to the Trademark protection, the Right Holder gets an opportunity to effectively protect their intellectual rights to the Trademark on the Platform in addition to the general legal mechanisms for trademark protection provided for in Chapter 76 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation.

2.4. The relations between the Right Holder and Ozon shall be governed by Article 429.4 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation on the subscription agreement.

# 3. Brand PA

3.1. To register and log in the Brand PA, the Right Holder shall pass Verification in accordance with the [Terms of Use of Ozon ID](https://docs.ozon.ru/legal/terms-of-use/site/ozon-id-terms/) or use a login and password. If Ozon ID is linked to the Brand PA, the Right Holder may be denied authorization using a login and password.

3.2. The Right Holder shall take all measures required to ensure the confidentiality of the Brand PA, and also guarantees its independent use without transferring access to third parties, except where such parties act on behalf of the Right Holder (in this case, the Right Holder shall be responsible for the actions of their authorized persons as for their own actions).

3.3. In the event of suspicions regarding the security of the Brand PA, Ozon ID, login and/or password due to their unauthorized use by third parties or malicious software:

(a) the Right Holder is obliged to immediately notify Ozon thereof via the support service;

(b) Ozon has the right to unilaterally change the password and/or terminate access to the Brand PA via Ozon ID, following which the Right Holder may contact the support service for re-authentication and restoration of access.

3.4. The Right Holder instructs Ozon to perform any actions in the Brand PA that are necessary for testing new types of services or functionality of the Brand PA, as well as processing the Right Holder's requests to the support service.

3.5. The Right Holder's actions in the Brand PA shall be performed by generating an electronic document in Ozon information systems. Such electronic documents (files, electronic protocols, journals, program codes, logs and other information in electronic form):

(a) shall be recognized as signed by the Right Holder using a basic electronic signature;

(b) shall be equivalent to paper documents signed by the handwritten signature of the Right Holder;

(c) shall give rise to legal consequences similar to the use of handwritten signatures in accordance with the laws of the Russian Federation.

3.6. Upon termination of the Agreement, Ozon shall delete the Brand PA and all information contained therein without the possibility of restoration.

# 4. Inspection of the Right Holder's Documents

4.1. The Right Holder shall upload documents to the Brand PA according to the [list](https://seller-edu.ozon.ru/account-brand/how-to-start), and Ozon shall check them for completeness, validity, and the Right Holder's eligibility for the Trademark protection. Ozon is entitled to request and the Right Holder is obliged to submit additional documents regarding the Trademark and/or the Right Holder's powers. Following the inspection, Ozon shall make a decision on granting the Right Holder access to the Brand PA.

4.2. In the events specified in the [list](https://seller-edu.ozon.ru/account-brand/how-to-start), Ozon requests from the Right Holder:

(a) notarized documents, including notarized copies of documents;

(b) notarized translations of documents into Russian, including with an apostille or consular legalization;

(c) documents authenticated by a seal;

(d) a notarized translation of the seal;

(e) documents issued by FIPS;

(f) documents confirming the powers of the Right Holder's Representative, including the authority to sign documents on behalf of the principal Right Holder.

4.3. If instructed by the Right Holder, Ozon shall corrects any errors made by the Right Holder when indicating the dates and numbers of the uploaded documents.

4.4. The duration of the Right Holder's Representative’s rights to protect the Trademark may not exceed the duration of the principal Right Holder’s rights to the Trademark.

4.5. Access to the Brand PA may be granted to one Representative of the Right Holder only or to the principal Right Holder. If several Right Holder’s Representatives apply for the access, access shall be granted to the Representative who first uploaded the documents for inspection. In case of discrepancies, the principal Right Holder shall send Ozon an official letter indicating the Representative who should be granted access to the Brand PA.

The principal Right Holder may also revoke the Right Holder’s** **Representative's access to the Brand PA at any time.

4.6. Access to the Brand PA in relation to the Trademark of an individual who is not an individual entrepreneur shall be granted to the Representative of such Right Holder.

4.7. Ozon has the right to conduct a repeated inspection in relation to the Trademark and/or the Right Holder’s powers, and the Right Holder shall ensure completeness and validity of the documents, as well as their conformity with the [list](https://seller-edu.ozon.ru/account-brand/how-to-start) and requirements of this Section. In the event of any discrepancies, Ozon shall notify the Right Holder, who shall have the right to provide up-to-date and/or missing documents within 5 business days. If documents are not provided, Ozon shall revoke access to the Brand PA, stating the reason. The Right Holder shall have the right to re-upload supporting documents, and Ozon shall be obliged to return access to the Brand PA to the Right Holder if there are grounds for doing so.

4.8. Ozon refuses to provide access to the Brand PA or revokes it at any time if:

(a) upon inspection of the Right Holder's documents (including repeated inspection), they are found to be incomplete, invalid and/or non-conforming to the [list](https://seller-edu.ozon.ru/account-brand/how-to-start) and requirements of this Section;

(b) the goods on the Platform do not comply with the classes of the Nice Classification in respect of which the Trademark is registered and/or in respect of which the Right Holder is entitled to protect the Trademark;

(c) the Trademark has a new Right Holder;

(d) the legal protection of the Trademark has been terminated or found to be provided to the Trademark with no valid reason;

(e) Ozon receives from the Right Holder an official letter about the revocation of the Representative's authority to protect the Trademark;

(f) the Right Holder is in the process of liquidation or reorganization or subject to any bankruptcy proceedings;

(g) for other reasons that Ozon reports upon the Right Holder's request.

# 5. Inspection of Disputed Product

5.1. Upon successful verification of the documents confirming the right to the Trademark, the Right Holder may:

(a) review the list of Sellers offering goods under the Rights Holder's Trademark;

(b) monitor the goods offered on the Platform under the Rights Holder's Trademark;

(c) file an appeal with Ozon if the Right Holder has doubts regarding:

the authenticity of the goods offered for sale on the Platform under the Right Holder's Trademark;

the Seller's right to sell the goods under the Right Holder's Trademark.

5.2. In their appeal, the Right Holder shall indicate the name of the Trademark and give the link to the Disputed Product of the Seller. Sending the appeal, the Right Holder confirms that the Disputed Product is protected by the Trademark owned by them and belongs to the class for which such Trademark is registered according to the International Classification of Goods and Services.

5.3. Ozon shall consider the Right Holder’s appeal and decide whether to conduct an inspection in relation to the Disputed Product. For the duration of the inspection, Ozon has the right to block the Disputed Product on the Platform.

5.4. During the inspection, Ozon requests documents from the Seller to confirm their right to sell the Disputed Product specified in the Right Holder's appeal. The Right Holder has the right to review the documents provided.

5.5. The Right Holder shall assist Ozon in inspecting the Disputed Product and/or the Seller, as well as give comments regarding the lawfulness of the Seller's offering the Disputed Product on the Platform.

5.6. After the inspection, Ozon shall notify the Seller of the decision taken:

(a) to block the Disputed Product and make it unavailable for sale on the Platform;

(b) unblock the Disputed Product and make it available for sale on the Platform again;

(c) leave the Disputed Product available for sale on the Platform.

# 6. Premium Brand Subscription

6.1. Upon completion of the document inspection and receiving access to the Brand PA, the Right Holder can use its benefits, provided that the Subscription is activated. To do this, the Right Holder should ensure payment and click the "Activate" button, whereupon information about its activation appears in the Brand PA. Ozon's obligation to render services under the Subscription shall be considered fulfilled at the time of its activation.

6.2. The Subscription access period shall be specified [via the link](https://seller-edu.ozon.ru/account-brand/podpiska-premium-brand). The absence of a request to cancel the Subscription and the possibility of writing off the payment shall mean the Right Holder's unconditional consent to the automatic renewal of the Subscription for the next access period. The number of automatic renewals of the Subscription is not limited.

6.3. The cost of the Subscription and additional services shall be indicated in the Brand PA. Automatic renewal of the Subscription shall be at the prices specified in the Brand PA, at the time of payment for the next access period. Ozon has the right to unilaterally increase the cost of the Subscription and/or additional services by giving a Notice to the Right Holder at least 7 calendar days before the changes come into force.

6.4. As payment for the Subscription, its automatic renewals, additional services, compensation for damages and pecuniary losses Ozon shall withhold funds from the Right Holder's balance (unless otherwise specified in the Agreement). To top up the balance, the Right Holder shall click on the account top-up button in the Brand PA and enter the amount by which the balance needs to be topped up. Ozon shall generate an invoice for payment of the specified amount and send it to the Right Holder. After the invoice is paid and the funds are received by Ozon, information about the remaining funds on the Right Holder's balance becomes available in the Brand PA.

6.5. The Right Holder instructs Ozon, by using technical means, to initiate and draw up, on behalf of the Right Holder, orders to write off funds from their balance to pay for the initial activation of the Subscription, its automatic renewals, additional services, compensation for damages and pecuniary losses. The Right Holder agrees to the automatic periodic writing off of funds from their balance to pay for automatic renewals of the Subscription and acknowledges that such writing off is made by order and with the consent of the Right Holder. The writing off of payment for automatic renewal of the Subscription does not require additional confirmation from the Right Holder.

6.6. The Right Holder is entitled to assign payment obligations under the Agreement to a third party, who is obliged to confirm their readiness to fulfill them. Within 5 working days after receiving such confirmation, Ozon ensures the activation of the Subscription, its automatic renewal and/or the provision of additional services to the Right Holder. In this case, the provisions of this Section on the procedure for topping up the balance, writing off funds from it and drawing up a Job Completion Report do not apply to the Right Holder.

6.7. The Subscription fee shall be paid regardless of the use of the Brand PA functionality during the access period.

6.8. Changes in the content of the Subscription and additional services provided to the Right Holder shall not be cause for canceling the Subscription for the current access period, revising its cost and/or duration, or paying back the funds.

6.9. The Right Holder has the right to refuse from automatic renewal of the Subscription by clicking the «Cancel Subscription» / "Disable Premium Brand" button in the Brand PA and then confirming their refusal. If you refuse from automatic renewal of the Subscription, the access to it shall be retained until the end of the current period.

6.10. No later than the 8th day of the month following the month in which the next access period expired, Ozon shall send the Right Holder a Job Completion Report, UPD-1 (hereinafter referred to as the Report), to the Brand PA. Within 5 working days after the Report is sent, the Right Holder either approves it or makes reasoned objections, upon receipt of which Ozon shall make the accepted amendments and send the amended Report to the Right Holder. In the absence of feedback from the Right Holder within the specified period, the version of the Report prepared by Ozon shall be considered approved.  If the Right Holder is not connected to the electronic document exchange system, they assume responsibility for all negative consequences arising therefrom, and sending the Report to the Brand PA shall be recognized as necessary and sufficient confirmation of proper fulfillment by Ozon of the obligation to draw up the Report and agree it with the Right Holder.

Due to technical reasons, the Subscription may be temporarily displayed in the report as «Premium Brand» or «Premium Plus».

6.11. Ozon has the right to deny activation of the Subscription and/or its automatic renewal, and immediately disable access to the Subscription without returning funds to the Right Holder for the current access period:

(a) upon termination of the Agreement at the initiative of Ozon due to violations by the Right Holder;

(b) if the Right Holder has violated the Agreement and/or the terms of contracts concluded with Ozon;

(c) if the third party charged with payment obligations under the Agreement refuses or loses the opportunity to fulfill them;

(d) if Ozon has revoked access to the Brand PA on the grounds stipulated by the Agreement.

6.12. For the Right Holder – Seller, the Subscription is a part of the Premium Plus or Premium Pro service. Subscription management, payment and sending documents are carried out in accordance with the [Contract for Ozon Platform Sellers](https://seller-edu.ozon.ru/contract-for-sellers/contract-goods/contract-for-sell-goods-on-ozon) (is not part of the Agreement except in cases specified in it).

# 7. Premium Brand Subscription Services

After activation of the Subscription, the Right Holder receives the right to:

(a) respond to reviews and questions from Clients;

(b) use additional tools to analyze sales of the goods offered for sale on the Platform under the Right Holder's Trademark;

(c) use other services available in the Brand PA.

# 8. Responses to Reviews and Questions from Clients

The Right Holder's responses to reviews and questions from Clients shall:

(a) be informative, relate only to the product being commented on and do not contain links to other products or the Right Holder's website;

(b) be drawn up in accordance with the [Ozon Rules for Communicating with Buyers](https://seller-edu.ozon.ru/policies/rules/communication).

# 9. Product Promotion

9.1. Regardless of activation of the Subscription, Ozon may provide the Right Holder with services for promoting products under the Right Holder's Trademark, including those posted on the Platform by other Sellers.

9.2. The Right Holder provides Ozon with advertising materials that comply with the [Requirements for Advertising Materials](https://seller-edu.ozon.ru/how-to-sell-effectively/media-advertising/trebovania-k-materialam). The Right Holder shall at their own discretion determine the location for each advertising material from among those available.

9.3. Ozon's remuneration shall be calculated based on the scope of promotion services provided to the Right Holder in a calendar month. The cost of Ozon's services shall include VAT.

9.4. Ozon shall issue an invoice for the promotion services provided to the Right Holder before the 15th day of each calendar month. The Right Holder shall pay for the promotion services by transferring funds to Ozon's bank account within 7 business days from the date of the invoice. The day when the money is credited into Ozon's current account shall be deemed the day of payment.

9.5. The Right Holder's payment obligations may be discharged in whole or in part by offsetting a counter-claim, including under other agreements with Ozon or money claims.

9.6. By agreement of the Parties, Ozon may provide the Right Holder with additional services for the promotion of goods and/or the production of advertising materials. The Right Holder shall send Ozon a request for such services through the Brand PA. If the Parties agree on the service parameters Ozon shall post information on the essential terms (cost, times and procedure for the provision of services) in the Brand PA in the «Advertising» tab, and the Right Holder shall accept them by clicking the "Confirm" button. By clicking the "Confirm" button, the Right Holder fully and unconditionally agrees to the terms of the services.

9.7. Ozon has the right to suspend its promotion services in the following circumstances:

(a) if determined that the advertising materials contain incorrect information or otherwise violate the requirements for goods and advertising materials established by the Agreement or the laws of the Russian Federation;

(b) for technical, technological and other reasons that prevent the provision of promotion services, for the time of eliminating such reasons.

9.8. The Right Holder is obliged to provide documents confirming compliance with the legal requirements of the Russian Federation, including the laws on advertising, with respect to the promoted goods within 3 business days from the date of receipt of the request from Ozon.

9.9. In accordance with Article 18.1 of the Federal Law dated 13.03.2006 No. 38-FZ "On Advertising", Ozon transfers information about the Right Holder's advertising to Roskomnadzor through an advertising data operator, and the Right Holder shall ensure that Ozon is provided with all the necessary information about its advertising. The list of the advertising information to be transferred is defined in the Order of the Government of the Russian Federation dated 30.05.2022 No. 1362-r.

9.10. If, when providing promotion services, Ozon creates intellectual property then the exclusive rights to such property shall belong to Ozon in full, unless otherwise provided by the Agreement.

# 10. Brand Storefront

10.1. The Rights Holder who has successfully passed the procedure of verifying documents confirming the right to protect the Trademark gains access to the Brand Storefront creation feature in the PA.

This functionality becomes available to the Rights Holder after the Brand Promotion service is activated by the Rights Holder or by any Seller in relation to goods marked with the Rights Holder's Trademark.
10.2. The Brand Storefront enables the Rights Holder to:
(a) select, from the Sellers who have activated the Brand Promotion service, which Sellers' goods will be displayed on the Brand Storefront on the Platform;
(b) create the Brand Storefront design for display to Clients on the Platform.

10.3. The content of banners and other Content on the Brand Storefront must not constitute advertising, as defined in Federal Law No. 38-FZ «On Advertising».

(a) confirms the accuracy of all information indicated in the brand page;

(c) expresses consent to place the information and content specified in the brand page on the Supplier Storefront;

(d) guarantees that in respect of the materials (images, texts, etc.) uploaded to the brand page, the Rights Holder has duly received:

the exclusive right or license to any IRPs and means of individualization contained therein;

all necessary consents to use the IPRs without specifying the name of the author and/or other persons who participated in their creation as well as to publish, modify, abridge, and complement, supply them in use with illustrations, a foreword, afterword, comments or any notes;

all necessary consents to use of trade and other names, trademarks, service marks, trade designations, images and/or other third parties’ IPRs, means of individualization and intangible assets protected;

other consents and permits, including from the holders of the respective materials and information contained therein, for the placement of such materials and information on the Supplier Storefront to the extent of this Agreement, if obtaining such consents and permits is provided for by applicable legislation.

11.4. The information specified by the Rights Holder in the brand page for placement on the Supplier Storefront must not constitute advertising, as defined in Federal Law No. 38-FZ «On Advertising».

11.5. Ozon has the right to deny the Rights Holder the creation of a brand page or to delete an already published brand page if its content contradicts the Agreement, the [Ozon Content Policy](https://docs.ozon.ru/legal/en/terms-of-use/site/content-policy/), applicable legislation, or infringes the rights of third parties.

(b) the content and reliability of the information contained in the advertising materials and its compliance with the requirements of the legislation of the Russian Federation;

(c) violation of the rights of third parties (including intellectual ones) caused by the use of advertising materials transferred by the Right Holder;

(d) the content of the Brand Storefront and any actions taken with the Brand Storefront.

12.3. The Right Holder protects Ozon from any claims and complaints from the Seller, third parties and/or governmental agencies related to the violation of their rights and/or the posting of the advertising materials.

12.4. The Right Holder undertakes to reimburse Ozon, the Seller and/or third parties for all damages and/or pecuniary losses incurred due to:

(a) unjustified blocking of the Disputed Product;

(b) violation by the Right Holder of the terms of the Agreement, as well as the rights and legitimate interests of the Seller and/or third parties;

(c) concealment of information about the rights holder of the Trademark from publicly available sources of Rospatent, as well as of transactions completed in relation to the rights to the Trademark;

(d) the occurrence of civil or administrative liability for Ozon due to the Right Holder’s and/or involved third parties’ actions (inaction);

(e) claims/lawsuits/demands brought against Ozon by third parties in connection with the placement by Ozon of a brand page created by the Rights Holder.

Damages and/or pecuniary losses under the Agreement are accrued and paid, including by deduction from the Right Holder’s balance, based on a Notice sent by Ozon to the Brand PA or to the Right Holder's e-mail. Upon termination of the Agreement, the obligation to compensate for damages and pecuniary losses remains in force with respect to the Right Holder’s actions (inaction) committed before the termination.

12.5. Claims against Ozon under the Agreement or in connection with it are limited to compensation for actual damage and do not include lost profits or consequential damages.

12.6. Ozon is not liable for non-fulfillment or improper fulfillment of its obligations under the Agreement:

(a) if the Right Holder has not informed Ozon of their claim within 30 calendar days from the moment when he learned or should have learned about the occurrence of the reasons for its lodging;

(b) if the Right Holder has provided Ozon with unreliable, incomplete, inaccurate or misleading information and/or documents;

(c) in connection with temporary technical problems / failures in the operation of the Brand PA and information systems, which do not constitute a waiver of obligations within the meaning of Articles 310 and 782 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation.

13.1. All documents and information provided by the Right Holder are valid, reliable, complete and not misleading when provided and during the term of the Agreement. The Right Holder shall immediately notify Ozon of any circumstances making it obvious that the documents and/or information provided is invalid, unreliable or incomplete.

13.2. The Right Holder has the organizational, financial, human, and other resources necessary for due performance of the Agreement; no other documents or information which could adversely affect Ozon’s opinion in assessing the potential risks associated with conclusion or performance of the Agreement exist, and the Right Holder is not aware of any facts, issues or any other circumstances the disclosure of which could affect Ozon’s willingness to enter into the Agreement with the Right Holder.

(d) there are no obstacles to performing the Agreement, including by virtue of the Articles of Association, corporate and other documents of the Right Holder, provisions of the law and the terms of transactions mandatory for the Right Holder;

(e) if the third party is charged with payment obligations under the Agreement – there is a valid contract between the Right Holder and the third party, under which the third party performs the payment obligations without transferring to him the rights under the Agreement;

The Right Holder agrees that Ozon may verify the authenticity and validity of the documents provided, including by making appropriate inquiries to the persons who issued such documents, are their signatories, or are indicated in them as contacts/authorized persons.
